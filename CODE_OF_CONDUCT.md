# Code of conduct

We want this project to be useful, rigorous, and welcoming.

Be respectful, discuss the technical claim rather than the person, provide evidence for capability
and availability corrections, and assume good faith while remaining precise. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, and publication of private credentials or data are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior puts people
or the project at risk. Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer channel rather than a
public issue when possible.
